Audio format: mono, 8,000 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV, 8 seconds each.
The notes follow semitone offsets 0,4,7,12,7,4,2,7 at two notes per second.
The six base frequencies start at 164.8138 Hz, one semitone apart. A decaying
sine wave with a quiet second harmonic and short attack/release shapes each note.
