## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done 2026-10-11 (parallel agent, worktree).

The table: Twilight Fair (assets/tables/twilight-fair), a funfair at dusk. Theme chosen over the abyss because it is the opposite of Tidal Lock on every axis the format carries (warm against cold colours, bells and a steam organ against synthetic space sounds), and because strings of bulbs use what ZN-649.10 brought: pools of light that overlap. Its folder name sorts after tidal-lock on purpose: the game and its tests take the first table of the assets.
Layout: the starter's arch, lane and flippers; slingshots; three top lanes with lights that the flippers rotate; a coconut shy (three drop targets); a spinner; the high striker (a kicker that throws the ball onto the bandstand, an upper playfield at 5 cm with a rail and two bells); 17 lights (seven bulbs under the arch, flashed by the bumpers), 8 models (cabinet, a big wheel and a ticket booth as OBJ files, a striped cone), 17 looks, 34 sounds, 24 rules, 4 missions, 5 ranks, both screen layouts with a backglass, credits.

Made by sessions (tools/twilight_fair/sessions/01..05, 4572 lines of pointer, keys and typed text, written by sessions.py with typist.py because no person was at the keyboard): everything of table.json. Starting point: next/tests/golden/pinball3d-editor/new.json. The test replays them and gets assets/tables/twilight-fair/table.json byte for byte.
Typed as the file has it (the honest measure of the editor):
- 2 rules with a condition (all lane lights on; all coconuts down), written in the editor's advanced field (Cmd+J): the form cannot build a lights/targets condition one field at a time, each state on the way is refused by the format's check (belongs to ZN-649.14).
- Nothing was typed in table.json outside the editor. No line of the game's src/ changed.
- Typed in forms rather than pointed at: places off the half-centimetre grid (bulbs, scenery), angles, the kicker's exit, colours as hex.
- Outside the editor: the 11 images and 2 models (tools/twilight_fair_art.py) and the 34 sounds (tools/twilight_fair_sounds.py, all synthesised), 715 KB.
The editor was fixed where the making found it wrong (own commit 68524090): a click on a line of a filtered list took the first line; Tab stopped on the colour swatch.

Proof (tests/t1/pinball3d_second_table.sh, new): 'pinball3d_second_table: ok': sessions = what sessions.py writes; replayed table = shipped table; loader accepts, canonical; the scripted game (play.input, the hands used on Tidal Lock) prints tests/data/pinball3d/twilight-fair.log (209 lines, 49 hits, a mission done, game over at 74,410); probe: 'striker capture', 'striker eject', 'bell1 hit', highest 6.4 cm; every file named exists and is credited, no file unused; no name or text of the table in the game's code. pinball3d (Tidal Lock, same code) still passes.
