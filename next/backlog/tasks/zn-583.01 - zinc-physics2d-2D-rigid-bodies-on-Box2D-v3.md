## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The 2D half of ZN-583, needed first by the 3D pinball (PB-*). A plugin `plugins/physics2d` on Box2D v3 (MIT), vendored with its licence, pinned version and checksum: world with a fixed step and sub-steps, static / kinematic / dynamic bodies with continuous collision for fast small bodies (a pinball), shapes (circle, capsule, polygon, chain of segments for walls), revolute joint with motor and limits (flippers), prismatic joint (plunger), contact begin/end events with the impulse, sensors, raycast, per-shape friction and restitution, deterministic stepping. Typed API for compiled Zinc code, the same calls from the interpreter and from QuickJS. No physics in the engine core: a plugin, loaded by programs that import it.

Decisions (RULES section 4)
- Library: Box2D 3.1.1, the latest release (2025-06-04, tag v3.1.1, archive sha256 fb6ef914...), named by the task and ZN-583. Kept unmodified: include/ and src/ without src/CMakeLists.txt and box2d.natvis.
- Include path for a vendored C library: the builder passes the manifest's flags to vendored .c files (1 line, any C library with include/ and src/ needs it) rather than moving the release's headers under src/ (changes the release layout, surprises the next update) or listing the 35 files (still needs -I). Scored 5/5/5/5/5/4/5 against 3/5/5/2/5/4/4.
- One unity C file (box2d.c): 4 s with clang, no name clash.
- -ffp-contract=off for the library, as Box2D's own CMakeLists does for its cross-platform determinism; NDEBUG turns its asserts off; SSE2 (no AVX2, so any x86_64 runs it).
- Shapes are descriptions on the TS side (kind + numbers), attached by world.body (first shape, its material in the body options) and Body.addShape (more shapes, each with its own friction, restitution, density, layer, mask, sensor, events): Box2D attaches shapes to one body, there is nothing to share or release.
- Collision filter: Box2D's own category/mask bits (layer 0..31, mask) instead of physics3d's pair table, which would need a custom filter callback in every pair test.
- Contact impulse: Box2D records a begin event before it solves (impulses zero), so the impulse is read after the step from the contact's manifold (sum of totalNormalImpulse); the speed is the largest pre-solve approach speed of its points.
- Joint motor: no unbounded torque. With 1e30 the flipper's motor fought its limit until the solve blew up (the blade jumped to -2.7 rad); Joint.motor takes the limit as a required argument and 0 is Box2D's own (no torque).
- Joints to the world use a static ground body made at the first such joint.
- One thread (no task callbacks): exact, nothing to manage on small targets.

Findings (in docs/plugins/physics2d.md)
- A body that moves into a continuous ball is seen at its end-of-step pose (Box2D sweeps the bullet against the final pose of the other body): with a revolute flipper at 40 rad/s, 1 of 2,500 balls through at 240 Hz, 0 at 480 and 960 Hz. The pinball should step at 960 Hz.
- Box2D never sweeps a bullet against another bullet: two balls at 40 m/s closing need the closing-speed rule (n = ceil(closing x dt / (0.5 r)) steps of dt / n): 0 of 2,500 through.
- A chain is one-sided and thin: in the 2,000-body pile a chain floor let bodies through (y down to -1008); the bench uses boxes, 0 out.
