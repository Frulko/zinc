## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done (2026-10-11). Zinc Next's devtools transport now answers Tracing: cdp.next.ts trace() reaches the runtime's profiler (zrt::gfx::trace, the spans ZINC_TRACE writes) through the inspector's host channel (host.netLog ops 7 start, 8 end; zn::host::traceEvents in src/host/gfx_host.cpp); before, it returned an empty trace. app/inspector: a Performance tab with Record and Stop (Tracing.start, Tracing.end, then the Tracing.dataCollected chunks until tracingComplete). It shows the time of each phase over the recording and the frames it ran in, the frames as rows of their phases to scale (VirtualList: an unthrottled headless program makes thousands of frames, and plain rows overflowed the 8192 draw commands), and the phases of the picked frame (the slowest at first) in the right column. model.ts: TraceFrame/traceFrames (the zinc frame spans of the main thread with the phase spans they hold, one sorted pass), PhaseTotal/phaseTotals, msText (µs under a millisecond). AC1: tests/t1/inspector.sh records half a second of tests/data/inspector and checks that frames were recorded, each with its phases up to present, the phases app, layout and present, and the slowest frame's phases. Screenshot checked by eye. The runtime keeps the last 32768 spans, about 3600 frames (a recording of 0.5 s headless hits it, a vsync window does not). Not done: Tracing against zinc debug --cdp (no frames there; the view stays at recording). usage: n/a
