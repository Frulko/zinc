## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
The agent starts apps with an inspector port and says which. `zinc device <d> inspect <app>` forwards it and prints the DevTools address; Zinc Inspector lists the apps of paired devices next to the local ones; `zinc device <d> shot <app>` is the app's own frame through the inspector protocol; `zinc device <d> monitor <app>` is the zinc:telemetry stream in the words of zinc monitor, and `zinc device <d> top` the machine's load, memory and temperature with each app's CPU and RSS every second. Replaces the ssh forwarding of ZN-373.06.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
