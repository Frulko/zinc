## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
After ZN-412.01, bouncing-ball's 200k balls replay in 1.4 ms of GPU but 3.6 ms of CPU: 2.9 ms of it zrt::raster::cull (8 px tiles, bitmask rows, 14 ns a command), 0.8 ms the passes over the 46k visible commands. Ideas: bounds kept from the frame diff or computed while the commands are pushed, the previous frame's visibility as a first guess, SIMD over the command array, culling only when the GPU is the slower side (timer queries).

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
usage: n/a

Done:
- runtime/raster.cpp: cull_list replaces cull. The walk writes the visible commands front to back, with kCullOpaque on the opaque ones outside clips. The GL replay then walks only that list: no hidden array, no pass over every command.
- The clip balance check folds into the walk: a CLIP met at depth 0 stops the culling, a full walk must end at depth 0, a short one checks the rest.
- cull_tile: tiles of 2/3 the mean size of the opaque rectangles (sampled in the last 4096 commands), 4 to 32 px. bouncing-ball's display-gl surface is 320x240: 4 px tiles leave 7.9k visible quads instead of 46k with 8 px.
- cull_pack (each command's tile range and kind in 8 bytes) runs on 4 threads in the GL plugin; cull_walk reads only the packed array. The memory-bound half is spread over the cores.

Measured, bouncing-ball 200k on display-gl, 1200 frames, two runs: GPU 0.656 / 0.671 ms (timer query), CPU submission 1.767 / 1.769 ms, culling 1.47 ms of it (AC1). Before this task: GPU 1.38 ms, CPU 3.65 ms.

Tried and dropped:
- A software prefetch in the walk: no gain.
- Bands of tile rows on 4 threads (ZN-412.01): slower.
- Sampling the tile size across the whole list: no gain against the last 4096 commands.

Tests: gl_occlusion, gl_renderer and tiles pass (AC2); bouncing-ball frames identical with and without culling (60 frames); tests/run --changed 4 passed.
