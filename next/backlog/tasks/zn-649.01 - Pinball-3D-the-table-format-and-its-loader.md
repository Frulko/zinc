## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
One file describes a table, and nothing about a table is in code: playfield size and slope; walls and lanes as polylines with arcs; guides and one-way gates; flippers (position, length, angle range, strength); plunger lane; bumpers, slingshots, kickers and holes, drop and standup targets, rollovers, spinners, ramps and upper levels as elevation layers; lights and their groups; for each element its physics (restitution, friction, kick force), its look (a primitive, or a mesh asset, material, colour, emissive) and its sounds per event; the rules as data (score per event, multipliers, missions with their steps and lights, ball save, extra ball); the camera. A loader that validates and reports errors with the path of the offending value, versioned, with a written reference. (Owner request, 2026-10-10: a pinball all in real 3D instead of fake 3D, with a suited physics engine, a vertical table in the style of Space Cadet, parametrable in its lanes and assets, with a complete editor to make new tables, visual and sound.)
