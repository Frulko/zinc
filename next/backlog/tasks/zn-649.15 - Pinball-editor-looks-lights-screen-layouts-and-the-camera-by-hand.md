## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done 2026-10-11 (parallel agent, worktree).

What changed (editor; one optional thing in the format)
- Colour: the swatch of a colour field opens a picker (form.tsx ColorField): a square of the shades of a hue, the strip of hues, and 'In this table', the colours the table already uses (doc.ts colorsIn walks the schema: up to 40). A drag across the square is one undo step. The hex text stays beside the swatch for a colour typed or pasted.
- Glow: the format gives light.glow the new role 'radius' (table-schema.ts ROLES: 'a distance around the thing's point'); the top view draws the circle of any field with that role and a round handle on it, and pulling the handle sets the distance (by 5 mm on the grid, 1 mm with Alt, within the field's limits). The editor still names no field: another kind that gets the role gets the handle.
- Upper playfields: the top view lays the image of a floor's look over the box around the floor, as the game does, under a veil (shapes.ts Outline.image).
- Screen layouts (new src/screen.tsx): while a layout or something in it is selected, the top view gives way to the game's window in that layout, drawn by the game's own code (View, scene, drawPanel) with the box of each thing of the panel; a click selects an item, a drag moves it, the square at its corner sizes it; a switch goes between landscape and portrait.
- Camera: in the 3D pane, while no game runs, a drag turns the eye around what it looks at, Shift and drag slides it over the table, the wheel comes closer, a button frames the whole board; 'Use this view' (key C) writes position and target into the layout the pane looks through, chosen by a switch in the pane (portrait by default: the pane is taller than wide).

Proof (tests/t1/pinball3d_editor_looks.sh, new; session looks.input; goldens looks.json, looks-52.png, looks-120.png)

Decisions
- The role, not the field name, gives the handle (D81: the editor knows no field). Only light.glow has it for now: a bumper's or a kicker's radius would take it too, but a handle on every round thing changes how a press on it is read; proposed as a follow-up.
- The layout preview uses a Game of its own without sound (a game waiting for a player), made again when the table changes.

Limits / follow-ups
- The painting of an upper playfield that is not a rectangle shows past its outline in the top view (zinc:gfx clips to boxes only).
- The fov of a layout's camera is still a number in the form; the table's view box and the panel's own box are not dragged yet (only the panel's items).
- Not verified: any of it by hand in a real window.
- affected.json to add: pinball3d_editor_looks for examples/pinball3d-editor/** and examples/pinball3d/src/**.
