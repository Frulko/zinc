## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
An object of the collection as a textured 3D model on the GPU renderer of zinc:3d: one finger turns it, pinch zooms, two fingers move it, with inertia and limits; points of interest on the model open their description and move the camera to them; lighting presets; a reset. Models load from the assets of the project (OBJ today; glTF if zinc:3d can load it, else a proposed follow-up). (Owner request, 2026-10-10: a demo app useful for mediation in museums: an interactive presentation on a touch table, photo gestures, 3D models, descriptions, presentations, in the manner of Klynt.)
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
