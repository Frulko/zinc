## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Every event of the table (flipper, bumper, slingshot, target, lane, launch, drain, mission step, tilt) plays the sound its element names in the table file, with volume and pitch variation, plus a music loop per game state, through zinc:audio (ZN-390). Original sounds: synthesised by a script kept in the example, or CC0 with the licence recorded. Silent headless. (Owner request, 2026-10-10: a pinball all in real 3D instead of fake 3D, with a suited physics engine, a vertical table in the style of Space Cadet, parametrable in its lanes and assets, with a complete editor to make new tables, visual and sound.)

Owner's answers, 2026-10-10: both kinds of sound: recorded mechanical sounds under CC0 (flippers, bumpers, the rolling ball) with their licences recorded, and synthesised effects and music from a script in the example. Pitch variation needs the pitch of zinc:audio (its own task in M27).

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done 2026-10-10 in a parallel worktree (branch worktree-agent-a52f6ccde0b6b258d). usage: n/a (a parallel agent).

What was built
- The game asks, the audio plays. src/game.ts leaves a Cue per sound in Game.cues (what made it, "pop1 hit"; the name the table gives; how hard, from the speed of the hit; where across the table), for every event of every element kind: wall hit, gate pass, flipper up and down, plunger pull and release, bumper, slingshot, kicker capture and eject, target hit and reset, rollover, spinner, ramp enter and exit, and the ball itself (roll, hit, land, knock). The sounds a rule asks for (`do: sound`) join the same list, which is how the 12 events of the game are heard. Game.rolling is the speed of the fastest ball that rolls.
- src/audio.ts (TableAudio): loads every sound of the table (a file of the table's source through zinc:audio decode, or a generated tone), plays the cues once a frame with the volume and pitch variation of the sound (a generator that repeats: one sound log per scripted game), pans by the place, moves the looped rolling sound with the ball (volume and rate), and changes the music with the state (attract, play, multiball, gameOver), the old one fading under the new one in 0.4 s. `preview(name)` and `seconds(name)` for an editor. Nothing in the code names a sound.
- The table: 42 sounds, 40 MP3 files, 594,884 bytes. Recorded under CC0 (12 files cut from six freesound.org recordings: a 1977 Gottlieb machine by schafferdavid, a marble on a table by NeatoEnt; each licence read on its own page, 2026-10-10): flippers, bumper, slingshot, plunger, drain, the ball rolling, landing and knocking, the dock's thunk. Synthesised by tools/tidal_lock_sounds.py (28 files): target, shields, lanes, gate, spinner, ramp, rail, dock, eleven jingles for the events of the game, four pieces of music in D minor (attract 76 bpm 12.6 s, play 112 bpm 17.1 s, multiball 138 bpm 13.9 s, game over 6.5 s). Sources, authors and licences in examples/pinball3d/LICENSES.md and in the table file (`credits`).
- tools/soundcheck.ts plays every sound of a table on the mixer without an output and names the silent ones; tools/probe.ts prints the cues with `sounds`.

Decisions
- MP3 (LAME through ffmpeg, 64 kbit/s mono; effects at 44.1 kHz, music at 32 kHz): of the formats zinc:audio decodes it is the one that fits the budget with a mature encoder on the machine (the 71.6 s of sound would be 5.1 MB as 16-bit WAV; FLAC is lossless and was not measured; the only Vorbis encoder here is ffmpeg's own, marked experimental), and it loops without a gap: the decoder skips the encoder's delay (measured: durations exact to the sample, 12.6316 s for 16 beats at 76, and the step across the loop point smaller than steps beside it).
- Cues and player apart: the game stays a library without sound (the editor embeds it and chooses to call TableAudio.update), and a scripted game has a sound log on the silent stand-in.
- Rattle limits: a ball riding the curved wall meets a new facet every few milliseconds, so one wall, and the ball's own knock, sound at most once per 0.12 s; the same sound twice in a frame plays once.
- A ball that flies does not roll: the rolling sound counts balls whose vertical speed is under 0.3 of their flat speed plus 10 cm/s.
- The jingle of a served ball is skipped under the jingle of a new game by a counter in the table's rules, not by code.
- Format (version 1, optional fields with defaults): physics.ball.sounds { roll, hit, land, knock }; credits [{ files, author, source, licence }]. For the editor: Field.role (point, path, direction, profile, rotation; ROLES in table-schema.ts) and Field.unit on 102 fields, printed in the reference; checkTable exported from format/table.ts.

Not done, proposed follow-ups

- "Pinball 3D: a sound for a bump of the table and for the tilt warnings" (size S): the format has no event for a nudge. AC: `nudge` is a game event rules can listen to.
- "Pinball 3D: the mix judged by ear" (size S, a person): levels were set from measured peaks and means, not listened to; the music was checked on spectrograms.
