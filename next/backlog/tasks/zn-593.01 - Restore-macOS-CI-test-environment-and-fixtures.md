## Implementation Notes

- GNU `timeout` was absent on the macOS runner, producing exit 127 in the
  fuzz regressions. The pinned macOS ARM QEMU additionally links Homebrew
  `pixman`, `libgcrypt`, and `sdl2`; `otool -L` confirms these paths. `libgcrypt`
  brings its `libgpg-error` dependency. SDL3 does not supply SDL2.
- The all-example harness now sets `ZINC_FAKE_CAMERA=1`, including when a real
  USB camera is attached. Its mocked boundary regression retains runtime-failure
  classification; the actual camera CLI fixture returns `1 OK of 1 entries`.
- The previous fixed-audit negative fixture disappeared through legitimate
  constant folding. A runtime integer-to-f64 multiplication in a recursive
  function is rejected with and without optimization; disabling the audit
  preserves output `3`. No verifier rules changed.
- The bundled deep-link fixture declared only `deep-link` while appending its
  readback log. Direct launch confirmed exit 101 with `EACCES`; adding the exact
  `fs:write:<temporary directory>/out.log` permission restores cold/warm checks.
- Window dumps gained `opaque`, `vibrancy`, and a launch-specific native id.
  The golden now checks the new fixed fields; the test requires two matching,
  positive native ids and normalizes only that id for comparison.
- The camera pixel fixture uses libgphoto2's existing `IOLIBS_PREFIX=ptpip`
  transport filter, excluding attached USB devices while preserving the
  recorded no-camera image. `ZINC_FAKE_CAMERA=0` keeps the fixture independent
  of caller environment. Focused `camera_pixels` passes with the original golden.
- Atelier's emulator was first provisioned during the captured UI session,
  causing installer stderr to enter its Output pane. The fixture now provisions
  it first and checks its real greeting. Both preserved warm-cache and fresh
  empty-toolchain-home replays match the original screenshot exactly.
- Focused `examples_status`, `fixed_verify`, `macos_deeplink`, `macos_window`,
  and `camera_pixels` tests pass. The initial T2 run recorded 251 passes,
  11 failures, and 6 skips before the fixes. Its final summary was disrupted
  by editing the runner while its shell was still reading the file. The
  failures were addressed with focused reruns, including both cold and warm
  Atelier pixel proofs and the ARMhf Hero cross-build. This does not claim a
  complete green T2 run. No complete-suite repeat is required by the owner's
  targeted-testing instruction; fresh remote confirmation remains pending.
