## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
examples/pinball3d runs a table file: the 3D scene is built from the table (playfield, walls extruded, elements as meshes, the ball as a lit sphere, lights that switch), a fixed camera looking down a vertical table as the 90s desktop tables did, the score panel at the side; physics by zinc:physics2d on the inclined plane at a fixed step, with elevation layers for ramps; flippers, plunger, nudge with tilt; scoring, missions and ranks from the rules data; attract mode; keyboard and touch input. Ships one original table in that spirit. Runs in the interpreter and AOT on the GPU renderer and headless on the software one. (Owner request, 2026-10-10: a pinball all in real 3D instead of fake 3D, with a suited physics engine, a vertical table in the style of Space Cadet, parametrable in its lanes and assets, with a complete editor to make new tables, visual and sound.)
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->
