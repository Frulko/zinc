## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
After ZN-592.01 zinc links libSystem and libc++ only and `zinc --version` costs 8.3-10 ms on the Intel Mac against 4.6 for an empty program and 5.3 for qjs on an empty file; jsonout interpreted is 10.6 ms vs qjs 6.9 (best of 40). What is left of zinc's own: ~700 binds to libSystem/libc++ (-no_fixup_chains gave nothing), 28 static initializers (main, plugin_build, policy, prof, rtcalls, project, modules, jsx, loop, mqtt, http, regexp, sock, sys_host, permissions, zrt, gfx, hal_dispatch, layout_host, shaped_gfx, native, mimalloc's _mi_process_init...), 1176 pages touched vs 448 (a 12 MB binary). Measure each (an initializer-free build, page-ins with vm_stat), then cut the largest: initializers made lazy (function-local statics), mimalloc's process init deferred to the first runtime allocation, fewer pages.
