## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done 2026-10-11 (parallel agent, worktree).

What changed
- Format: two game events a rule can listen to, `bump` (the player bumps the table; emitted before a possible `tilt`) and `letter` (a letter of the initials is taken with the plunger or start). Tidal Lock plays `cabinetKnock` (sounds/nudge.mp3) and `letterKey` (sounds/letter.mp3), both synthesised by tools/tidal_lock_sounds.py and credited in the table file.
- Pools of light: one additive mesh per light (zinc:3d Blend.Add, vertex colours, ZN-696), the light's colour times (1 - d/glow)^1.5 x 0.8, on the level the light lies in (the board, or the upper playfield at its z), cut to the outline of that level by clipping the disc against the triangles of the outline (geometry.ts discWithin, any outline shape). Pools add up where they overlap.
- Decision: Tidal Lock is MIGRATED to additive pools (its board look no longer names a textureLit, art/playfield-lit.jpg is deleted, 208 KB less, tools/tidal_lock_art.py no longer paints it) AND look.textureLit KEEPS WORKING for a table that names one (the pool of a playfield light is then a disc of that image, as before; checked in pinball3d.sh on a copy of the table). Why both: the field costs 8 lines, the editor's test table still names it, and a painted lit board can show what an added colour cannot.
- Tidal Lock's glows were sized so that pools never met; they are now wider (lanes 3, ranks 2.4, locks 2, arrows 4.5 to 6, the four general lamps 8, the two beacon lights of the deck 2.4): the general illumination spills around the slingshot plates, and neighbours overlap. No body moved: glow is drawing only.

Proof
- AC1: tests/data/pinball3d/play.log is the old log plus 5 lines (2 x 'game bump' at 11.333 and 29.667, 3 x 'game letter' at 42.657); sounds.log is the old one plus 'rule: cabinetKnock' x2 and 'rule: letterKey' x3 and the count 'audio null: 44 sounds' (the two sounds have no variation, so the generator of the others is not shifted). pinball3d_sound requires a rule with a sound for bump and letter.
- AC2: golden landscape-12.png / portrait-12.png show beaconLight1 lit on the deck with its pool; tests/data/pinball3d/pool.ts prints 'inside 1.00 edge 0.50 corner 0.25 inner corner 0.75 outside 0.00' (the disc cut to an L-shaped level).
- Goldens rewritten with tests/t1/pinball3d.sh --update and pinball3d_sound.sh --update: the six frames (pools are now added light, wider), play.log, sounds.log. tools/tidal_lock_art.py run again: every other file of art/ is byte-identical.
- PASS pinball3d_format, pinball3d, pinball3d_sound, pinball3d_aot, pinball3d_editor, pinball3d_editor_table.
