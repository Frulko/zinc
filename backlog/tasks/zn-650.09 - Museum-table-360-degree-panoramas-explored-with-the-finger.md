## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
(Owner's answers, 2026-10-10, for the showcase demos of M27.) A media type for equirectangular panoramas (a museum room, an archaeological site): one finger looks around with inertia, pinch changes the field of view, points of interest in the panorama open descriptions or lead to other sequences. Rendered with zinc:3d on the GPU.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done 2026-10-10. examples/museum-table/src/panorama.ts: PanoramaView (zinc:3d, the inside of a 64x32 sphere, unlit texture, u right as the yaw grows; drag with inertia, zoom by pinch or wheel between 30 and 100 degrees, glideTo a point's view, project(direction) with the camera's own basis; directions y up, -z the middle of the image, +x a quarter turn right). src/pano.ts: the viewer until the sequence player embeds it: zinc run examples/museum-table/src/pano.ts -- [medium id] [language]; points drawn on their direction with their titles, a tap opens a card (title, text, a button to the point's sequence), the view log prints look / zoom / open / link / close on one line each.
Format (shared with the player and editor agents): Point.to (optional sequence id, validated as a reference, written when set, docs/museum-table-format.md: the row and the panorama direction convention).
