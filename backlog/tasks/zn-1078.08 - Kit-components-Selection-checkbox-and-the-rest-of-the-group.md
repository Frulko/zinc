## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Implemented the controlled Selection family in `selection.tsx`, reusing the kit switch/slider, Nuxt choice conventions, theme, icons, focus and native-control providers. Checkbox supports mixed/disabled/description; radio lists/cards support disabled choices, icons, badges, descriptions and columns; chips toggle independently or remove; rating clears and supports read-only. Existing Slider/Switch prop contracts and the default slider rail handle are preserved. Slider adds range, vertical/logarithmic mapping, markers, formatted value, bounds and keyboard steps; snapping starts at min. One gesture enum/transition replaces down/dragging flags. State changes cut to their final state under reduced motion. No dependency or import-time registration.

Keyboard proof includes radio skip/wrap/roving focus, slider Shift/Home/End and no-crossing range bounds, chip/rating actions, mixed/disabled controls, touch scroll cancel→second drag, and pointer snapping with nonzero min. Fixed Actions' shared disabled collector once with `ui.isDisabled`; both callers now skip a disabled middle item without losing value/focus parity, and the existing single-choice wrap is retained.

AppKit accepts/reports mixed state and restores the ordinary two-state click cycle after the app accepts a value. Native names use existing axlabel attributes. The existing headless controls dump was refreshed for focusable labelled switches, named controls and the 30px slider rail; every event/state result remained unchanged. No public test-only API. The natural dynamic grab and Show mapped-child code required frontend fixes ZN-1082/ZN-1083, integrated by the coordinator; no component workaround.

Research: [CUI core](https://github.com/yappologistic/CUI/blob/master/docs/components.md), [extended controls](https://github.com/yappologistic/CUI/blob/master/docs/components-more.md); WAI [checkbox](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/checkbox/), [switch](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/switch/), [radio](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/radio/), [slider](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/slider/).
