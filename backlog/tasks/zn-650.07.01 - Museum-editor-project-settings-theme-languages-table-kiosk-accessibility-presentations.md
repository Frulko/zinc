## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done (parallel worktree, 2026-10-11). Test: `next/tests/run --tier t1 --only museum_editor` -> PASS museum_editor. Usage: n/a (next/tools/usage prints n/a in a worktree).

What exists
- `editor/settings.tsx`: the settings of the project, opened by a click on the project's name in the top bar, by "Project settings" in the panel of the storyboard, or by Ctrl / Cmd + comma. Five sections at the left, the form of one in the middle, a live sample at the right.
  - Project and languages: the name of the installation in every language (its id in the advanced mode); the languages, the first being the default; a language is added by a button (Deutsch, espanol, italiano, Nederlands, portugues) or by its code; removed with a question that says how many texts go with it; the default language stays.
  - Theme: the six colours (a swatch, what each is for, its #rrggbb text); the two fonts and the logo as fields, with the note that the table does not apply them yet.
  - Table and kiosk: visitors, reach (a shorter reach brings the buttons back within it in the same step), the attract and reset delays, no way out of the app, statistics, the update address.
  - Accessibility: the text sizes (one to five), the six colours of the high-contrast mode; the sample wears them while this section is open.
  - Guided visits: the list, a new one named at once; its title in every language, its path (steps dragged by their grip, removed, added from the list or the map of sequences), whether it is the attract loop, delete.
- The live sample (`editor/preview.tsx`): a third player on the document as it is, showing the selected sequence, else the start; made again 0.25 s after the last change; it answers to the mouse.
- `editor/doc.ts`, one operation each, with lines in selftest.ts: setProjectTitle, addLanguage, removeLanguage (textsIn), setColour, setThemeFile, setNumber, setFlag, setUpdateUrl, setAttract, setTextSizes, addPresentation, renamePresentation, deletePresentation, addStep, removeStep, moveStep. The 50 mixed operations include them (24 kinds).
- A language more is a column more: the columns of the wide panels share its width (`LangColumns` takes a width that follows the number of languages), and the content language switch of the top bar grows with them.
- The question "where does it lead?" says who asks (`pickFrom`): a link's own sequence is not offered, a point of interest or a step of a visit is offered every sequence; for a step it asks "Which sequence comes next?".

Decisions (for the coordinator to file)
1. The sample is the real player, not a drawing of it: what the form shows is what the table will show, and the high-contrast colours are checked the same way.
2. Removing a language removes its texts from the document (undo brings them back): the file would drop them at the next save anyway, and the question can say how many.
3. The default language cannot be removed and languages are not reordered: every required text exists in the first one; changing the default needs a check of every required text (follow-up).
4. A shorter reach moves the buttons that fall outside it, in the same undo step: every operation leaves a file the loader accepts.
5. The settings open from the project's name and the storyboard's panel, not from a new button of the top bar, which has no room left in French.
6. The test picture is now the Great Wave of the sample instead of a golden frame of the same test: a change of the editor's look no longer redraws the picture inside six other goldens.

Limits
- The fonts and the logo of the theme are plain fields: the table does not apply them yet (src/type.ts), so the sample cannot show them.
- Text sizes are kept, not shown: the player has no text-size control yet (ZN-650.10).
- The table's width and height are not offered: changing them does not rescale what lies on the tables.
- A new sequence, point or guided visit is named in English ("Tour 1") in every language but French, so a third language shows that word instead of "Not translated yet".

Not verified: no real window (headless only); the forms in French were looked at for the project section only.
