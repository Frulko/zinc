## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done (2026-10-22, parallel worktree).

What: lib/std/stage.ts is the std module zinc:stage (row in kStdModules, src/frontend/modules.cpp), holding music-tree's core/display.ts and core/input.ts: DisplayObject, Stage, the row/column layout, the hooks (onAddedToStage, onRemovedFromStage, onUpdate, onMeasure, onDraw), tap and drag zones, scroll boxes, the hit test (findTopmostZoneAt), uiState. Added where it costs nothing: Sprite (same class), numChildren, getChildAt, getChildIndex, addChildAt, removeChildAt, contains (AS3 semantics: the node or a descendant; music-tree's one use is unchanged by it), stage, mouseX/mouseY (local), Stage.color. Added for anyone who has no gestures of their own: routePointer(x, y, isDown, dt) (music-tree's router without the carousel: tap, drag phases 0-2, scroll past 12 px with a fling) and Stage.frame(dt) (pointer, wheel, onUpdate, flings, then layout and drawing only when dirty, else keep()). music-tree keeps its own router in main.ts (carousel, clock faces, screen power, the notification row that yields to the carousel).

Decisions (no number taken; for the coordinator): (1) uiState keeps its name: the T1 test music_tree_sonos patches main.ts on 'uiState.dirty = false', and the criterion says its tests stay unchanged. (2) Touch zones are pooled (GUIDELINES says zones are reused, music-tree allocated one TouchZone per zone per drawn frame and a closure per drag zone): TouchZone gains originX/originY and drag(screenX, screenY, phase), the drag wrapper closure is gone; a router that keeps the pressed zone across frames copies it at the press (copyFrom), as routePointer and music-tree's main.ts now do. (3) No event system (addEventListener): hooks plus zones, as music-tree does. (4) VBox/HBox/Spacer/ScrollContainerNode stay in music-tree's components/layout-nodes.ts (they hold its colours and fonts next to the generic ones).

Tests: tests/t0/stage.sh (golden tests/golden/stage: layout positions, lifecycle order, AS3 names, hit test, tap, moved press, drag in local coordinates, scroll clip and scroll, no drawing on idle frames) PASS on the interpreter and QuickJS; the same program on AOT (--native) prints the same golden (not in the T0: the first native build of a version is slow). music_tree_sonos PASS (idle Rooms page draws at most 2 frames, Actualiser over the scrolled list, Reprendre ici). std_surface, modules, module_resolution, platform_files, affected_paths PASS. zinc check examples/music-tree: exit 0.

Before/after: a deterministic headless session of music-tree with no network (every URL of the app, zinc:sonos and zinc:spotify rewritten to 127.0.0.1:9; a carousel swipe and back, every page tapped, a wheel, three scroll drags with fling), ZINC_FRAMEHASH=1 over 900 frames: interpreter before = after on all 900 frames (105 picture changes); two before runs are identical. On QuickJS the frames 150-203 and 207-900 are equal before and after; frames 70-149 and 204-206 (the gsap carousel settle) differ between two runs of the unchanged source, so they prove nothing either way. A first try with the network on found the owner's real Sonos system by SSDP (read only: the presses that landed on its rows moved past 12 px, so no tap and no volume; nothing was changed on the speakers).

Docs: docs/reference/stage.md (every class, field, method, hook, the frame, what AS3 has that this has not; its example runs: a tap turns the ball blue), linked from docs/learn/README.md, examples/GUIDELINES.md and music-tree's README; CHANGELOG line.

For backlog/later.md: Cross-platform pass: music-tree on the HP TouchPad and the Nexus 7 with zinc:stage (taps, drags, scroll, idle frames, a frame's cost with tools/zbench), unchanged expected. Later: QuickJS under ZINC_DETERMINISTIC is not frame-deterministic for music-tree's gsap carousel (two runs differ at frames 70-149), which keeps a per-frame hash from proving QuickJS runs. Later: VBox, HBox, Spacer and a ScrollContainer node in zinc:stage (music-tree's are mixed with its colours), and a stack of scroll clips (one level today).

What AS3 has that zinc:stage has not (docs/reference/stage.md lists it): events (addEventListener, MouseEvent, ENTER_FRAME, bubbling); per-node scaleX/scaleY/rotation/alpha/filters/blendMode/mask/cacheAsBitmap; the graphics object; localToGlobal/globalToLocal, getBounds, hitTestPoint/hitTestObject, setChildIndex, swapChildren, getChildByName, mouseEnabled/mouseChildren/buttonMode; TextField, Bitmap, Loader, Sound nodes; multitouch. Usage: tools/usage printed n/a in this worktree.
