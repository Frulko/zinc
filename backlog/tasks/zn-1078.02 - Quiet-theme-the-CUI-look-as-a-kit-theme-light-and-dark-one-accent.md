## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done 2026-11-09 (worktree agent).

What it is: the kit's own mechanism, not a second one. Quiet = a fifth platform look 'quiet' (lib/std/platform/tokens.ts QUIET: CUI's control heights 30/36/44, radii 10/14/18, 13 px body, 36/28/22/17 headings, 36x20 switch, segmented track) plus the Quiet colours written into the kit's light and dark schemes (lib/std/kit/theme.ts quietColors). `useQuietTheme(accent)` selects both; `setThemeAccent(name)` swaps the accent (neutral, violet, blue, green, amber, orange, coral, rose: CUI's values) through platform `setAccent(light, dark, onTint)`, which became dark-aware. The schemes are now defined lazily at the first `theme()`/`setTheme()`: the kit has no top-level `ui.defineScheme` any more (ZN-1078.03 needs that).
- Springs: lib/std/kit/motion.ts (`SPRING_STATE/SMOOTH/SNAPPY/MORPH`, `springAt`, `springValues`, `springTo` over `ui.animateSeries`); duration + bounce, zeta = 1 - bounce, closed form; under `ui.prefersReducedMotion()` the series is the end value only (a cut).
- Components that read the look: Card (radius, padding 24, title/description sizes), Input (field radius, height, size), popover/menu/dialog/toast (panel and dialog shadows), Button (no resting shadow under quiet), Progress (6 px track). Every size is built at run time (`tx`), never a literal `text-[Npx]`: the baker bakes every literal size it finds in the sources (src/res/res.cpp scanFile), and a literal in a look nobody uses changed glyph rendering of the other looks. Checked: examples/ui/kit-gallery is byte-identical to develop under auto, macos, ios and android.
- Templates: dashboard and desktop-app now use zinc:ui/kit with `useQuietTheme()` (READMEs in five languages, template.json). `zinc check` and `zinc test` pass on both, both render.
- Docs: docs/ui-kit.md "The Quiet theme", docs/ui.md (the fifth look), CHANGELOG UI/New.

#1 Gallery: docs/img/design/src (PICTURE=gallery, SCHEME=dark, ACCENT=blue), shots docs/img/design/quiet-gallery.png, quiet-gallery-dark.png, quiet-gallery-blue.png, next to CUI's docs/images/components.png and components-dark.png. Same layout, same components, same values. What differs:
- weight: CUI sets titles and labels in Inter Medium (500); only Regular and Bold are baked, so `font-medium` renders Regular and our titles look lighter.
- card titles: CUI 13 px medium, ours 15 px (the kit's CardTitle lead size); the column ends about 25 px lower.
- avatars: CUI tints them per person with a presence dot; ours are grey (no tinted Avatar or presence badge in the kit yet: ZN-1078.12).
- reviewer roles: CUI shows select buttons with a chevron; ours are outline buttons (Select trigger in ZN-1078.06/08).
- Add button: icon left of the label in CUI, right in ours.
- coverage ring: CUI draws a grey track under the arc; ours draws the arc only.
QuickJS renders the gallery as the interpreter does.

#2: `kit_quiet` T0 (tests/t0/kit_quiet.sh): the look, card colour, radius, field height, the tint before and after `setThemeAccent('violet'/'blue')`, the dark card, a spring that lands, one value under reduced motion. The blue shot shows the accent switch.

#3: both `zinc new` templates.

Research (sonnet sub-agent, ZN-1078.01 notes; not re-fetched): SwiftUI duration+bounce springs, closed-form damped spring (ryanjuckett.com/damped-springs), web.dev prefers-reduced-motion (cut, keep feedback), shadcn neutral and Radix 12-step scales (we follow CUI's OKLab greys instead).

Decision (proposed for the coordinator, no number taken): the Quiet theme is a platform look plus kit scheme colours, chosen by `useQuietTheme`; not a separate theme system. Why: the looks already carry shapes and type, the schemes carry colours and `dark:`; one switch point, and a program that never calls it pays nothing.

Later.md lines:
- ZN-1078.02: `items-baseline` is refused (Z0005 UI-07); CUI aligns a stat and its delta on the baseline, we use items-end. Yoga supports baseline alignment.
- ZN-1078.02: Inter Medium (500) is not baked; `font-medium` renders Regular. CUI's look relies on 500 for titles.
- ZN-1078.02: zinc.json `platformLook` does not take 'quiet' yet; only `useQuietTheme()` in code selects it.
- ZN-1078.02: icon names passed through a variable are not compiled in (U0001); the gallery lists them in zinc.json "icons". A note in the error could name that key.
