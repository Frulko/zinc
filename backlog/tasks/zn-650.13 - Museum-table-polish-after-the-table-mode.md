What was built (examples/museum-table/src):

- `player.ts`: the attract loop has its own 3D views (`shows`), so it no longer resets the object and the panorama of the visit it sets aside: a visitor finds them as they left them (the model and the image are loaded twice when the loop shows them).
- `player.ts`: a project with more than two guided visits has one button, `Visites guidées`, which opens their list above it (story: `presentations open|close`); a press elsewhere closes it, and so does the reset.
- `--controls` lines end with `over=<n>`: how many controls, the credit of a full view among them, the subtitle lies on. museum_access now asks for `over=0` on every line of its sessions (four sides, three text sizes).
