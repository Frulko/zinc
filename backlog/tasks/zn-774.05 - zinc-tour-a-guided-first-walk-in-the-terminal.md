Done (2026-10-22, parallel worktree).

- src/cli_tour.cpp (cli_core.cpp only gains the command row, two help examples, tour in "Start here", and docs() made public in cli_core.h). zinc tour [step] [--list] [--reset] [--auto [--until step] [--dir path]].
- The eight steps of the audit's design (docs/reports/onboarding-cli-audit.md) and their checks: 1 the zinc new question with the game template chosen (it asks the folder's name; the new folder is the one whose zinc.json is newer than the question), checked by zinc.json and src/main.ts; 2 zinc run with its window at a terminal (closing it comes back), 3 headless frames under --auto, checked by its exit status; 3 the template's text gone and zinc check clean (the tour watches the file's stamp, Enter checks at once); 4 zinc check reports an error code (Z0101 for isDwon), the tour runs zinc explain on that code, then the fix checks clean; 5 the tour starts zinc dev (log in build/tour-dev.log), waits for "v1 refreshed" (or "v2 started" when the save came before the program ran), then stops it (SIGTERM, 2 s, then the group); 6 the link to docs/start/choose and the zinc new question beside the project (--auto: <project>-cli), checked by zinc check in the new project; 7 the lessons (docs/learn), the guide, zinc help and zinc explain, the lessons opened in the browser at a terminal; 8 Zinc Atelier (/Applications or ~/Applications/Zinc Atelier.app, bin/zinc-atelier on Linux) opened on the project with open -a ... --args (Atelier opens its first argument as the sketch), never under --auto; not installed: the install.sh --atelier line.
- Decided: steps 7 and 8 have nothing on disk to check; their check is the opener's exit status (browser, open -a), and under --auto or without Atelier they are done when shown. The tour project is made where the person is (as zinc new), not in ~/zinc-tour: the zinc new question already asks the name; --auto defaults to ./zinc-tour.
- Progress: $ZINC_HOME/tour.json {"project", "done", "version"} written after each step; zinc tour goes on at the first step not done, zinc tour 5 goes to a step (step 1 first when there is no project), --list ticks, --reset forgets (the project stays), a project that moved counts no step. Without a terminal or under CI it prints the steps and how to start, exit 0, and never reads stdin.
- --auto: ZINC_HEADLESS=1 for every child, the edits made by the tour, each check retried for 15 s, exit 1 at the first failing step with the reason.
- Text: 66 cli.tour.* / cli.cmd.tour / cli.example.tour.* keys in i18n/{en,fr,es,it,de}/cli.json, glossary terms (visite guidée, recorrido guiado, tour guidato, Tour; vous, tú, tu, du); i18n-check 0 errors, 0 warnings. The output of zinc check shown by the tour is its first line and its closing line, the project path cut.
- Proof: tests/t1/tour.sh (4 s): --auto --until 3, then --auto goes on from 4 (Z0101 and its explanation, the fix, Fast Refresh, the second project, tour.json 1..8, no zinc process left); at a pty: step 1 through the zinc new question then q, step 4 waiting for saves made by the test then q; CI=1 at a pty and stdin from /dev/null never wait; --list, step 9 refused, French list, a moved project, --reset. Also passed: i18n, first_run, cli_core.
