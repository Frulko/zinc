## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Matcher: 32-point cloud, greedy weighted nearest-point distance, 7 start points both ways, early abandoning (the $P core with $Q's abandoning; the $Q lookup-table lower bound is skipped, 160 templates need none: 1-2 ms per group). Held-out synthetic writers: letters 335/336, shapes 223/224, TS and C++ agree to 1e-9, same lines on interpreter, AOT and QuickJS. Templates are generated by tools/ink-train/make-templates from the train writers. Real-writer accuracy is unmeasured (tablet pass). Also fixed: native-gen wrote the C export of a Promise<T> as 'P' without T (DIs>P), refused at load; now DIs>Ps, tested in tests/t0/native_gen.sh.
