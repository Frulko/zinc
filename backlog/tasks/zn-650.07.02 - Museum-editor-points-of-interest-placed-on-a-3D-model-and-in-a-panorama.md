## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
(Split of ZN-650.07 proposed by its agent after the storyboard and the sequence editor were done; the editor is `examples/museum-table/editor/`, one operation per feature in `doc.ts` plus a line in `selftest.ts`.) The editor embeds the player's object viewer and panorama view (`src/viewer.ts`, `src/panorama.ts`, `src/orbit.ts`): a click on the model or in the panorama places a point of interest, which is then named, described in each language, given the view the visitor is taken to and, optionally, the sequence it leads to; points are moved and deleted the same way. Also: items turned on the stage with zinc:gfx setTransform, which exists now (rotation was a number in the advanced mode only).

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done (parallel worktree, 2026-10-11). Test: `next/tests/run --tier t1 --only museum_editor` -> PASS museum_editor; museum_format, museum_viewer, museum_panorama PASS after the additions to src/. Usage: n/a (next/tools/usage prints n/a in a worktree).

What exists
- `editor/points.tsx`: a 3D model or a panorama fills the middle of the window (opened by a double click on its item on a table, or "Place points of interest" in its panel), shown by the table's own views (`src/viewer.ts` ObjectView, `src/panorama.ts` PanoramaView). A drag turns the object or looks around (no inertia: a precise tool), the wheel comes closer; a click on the object places a point where the line of sight meets it; a click on a point selects it, a drag moves it, Delete removes it. Points are numbered as in the panel's list; those on the far side of the object are faint rings.
- The panel (`inspector.tsx` PointsPanel, PointPanel): the points of the medium; a point's name and what visitors read, in every language side by side; "Use this view" (what the author sees now becomes the view the point opens with) and "Show it" (glides there); the sequence its card leads to, chosen in the list or on the map, or none; the three numbers of its place in the advanced mode. Without a selected point: how the medium is first seen (same two buttons), the lighting of a model, and in the advanced mode the medium as the file has it.
- `editor/pick.ts`: the triangles of an OBJ text and where a line meets them (Moller and Trumbore, every triangle tried: 12,000 for the vase).
- `editor/doc.ts`, one operation each, with lines in selftest.ts: addPoint, movePoint, setPointText, setPointView, setPointTarget, deletePoint, setMediaView, setMediaLight; addMedia takes the texture of a model (the picture of the same name in the folder, found by state.ts). The 50 mixed operations of the self-test now include them (21 kinds).
- The table of a sequence (`editor/sequence.tsx`): an item is drawn turned (zinc:gfx setTransform; its zinc:ui box carries the same `transform: rotate`, so the pointer meets it where it is drawn), the round handle above the selected item turns it (whole degrees, Shift: steps of 15, upright within 2 degrees), the size corner and the hotspots turn with it.
- Player additions (src/): `ObjectView.ray(px, py)` (the inverse of `locate`), `PanoramaView.unproject(...)` (the inverse of `project`); `mediaJson` and `sequenceJson` exported by project.ts (Doc.jsonOf uses sequenceJson; a link is still cut out of its sequence's text).
- The launch directory and the locale come from zinc:sys (ZN-687): the PWD and LC_ALL / LANG workarounds of main.tsx, selftest.ts and strings.ts are gone.
- `--log` on the editor's command line prints where each point is put and where it is drawn.

Decisions (for the coordinator to file)
1. The place under a click is found in the editor (triangles read from the OBJ text) from a line of sight the player's viewer gives (`ray`): zinc:3d has no ray cast and gives no depth back, and the camera's arithmetic stays in one file.
2. A place is kept with four digits of its largest coordinate, a view in whole degrees (distance two decimals): the files stay readable, and the point is drawn within a pixel of the click.
3. A new point opens with the view it was placed from; "Use this view" replaces it. Selecting a point does not move the author's view (one adjusts from where one is); "Show it" glides to it.
4. Items on the table are drawn by one function under a matrix, at every angle, instead of zinc:ui boxes that cannot be painted turned (ZN-361.01): the frames of the table changed by a few hundred pixels (text and frame edges), goldens regenerated.
5. A model's texture is the picture of the same name beside it, taken when the model enters the library (guided; a field for it belongs to ZN-650.07.04).

Limits, each marked `ponytail:` in the code
- points.tsx: a glTF or GLB model has no triangles in the editor: its point goes on the plane through the middle of the object that faces the author (the advanced mode has the numbers). Needs a ray cast or the depth of a pixel in zinc:3d.
- pick.ts: every triangle is tried on each click.
- state.ts textureOf: by name only.
- The miniature of a table on the storyboard still shows its items upright.

Found
- The library and the table show a 3D model by its texture image (the UV atlas of the scan), which reads badly; a rendered thumbnail of the object would be right (camera.render inside beginImage / endImage).
- A panorama's unreadable or oversized picture gives "This file cannot be shown" in the points view; imageFromBytes still refuses pictures above 2^24 pixels.

Not verified: no real window (headless only); models other than the sample's OBJ vase (no glTF model was opened in the editor); the time of a click on a larger model.
