## Implementation Notes

Worked by a parallel agent (worktree), 2026-10-11, one commit.

What was made
- The role `radius` on a bumper's and a kicker's `radius` (table-schema.ts; the reference printed again: three lines). The top view already showed a circle with a round handle for a field of that role (a light's glow): a bumper and a kicker now have it, and pulling it sets the radius within the format's limits, by half centimetres on the grid.
- screen.tsx: beside the things of the panel, the panel's own box and the box the table is drawn in (the layout's `view`) are taken by a click, moved by a drag and sized by the square at their corner (the smallest box under the pointer is the one taken; the table's box wears its name and the field of view when chosen). The wheel over the table's box narrows or widens the eye's field of view, a degree a notch, the notches that follow one another being one undo step.
- topview.tsx: the painting of an upper playfield that is not a rectangle is shown inside its outline, under a veil of the same outline.

A path clip in zinc:gfx: what is missing
- zinc:gfx clips to a box, with rounded corners at most (`clip(x, y, w, h, radius)`); there is no clip to a path and no image drawn through a polygon. It was not added: it is a change of the raster and of the GL renderer, the shared core, which a parallel worktree does not touch. The editor draws the image in strips one pixel high, each cut where the outline crosses the middle of that row (topview.tsx drawInside): exact to the pixel, no smoothing of the edge, as many draws as the outline is tall. A rectangle keeps the single draw, so the frames of the earlier tests are the same bytes.
