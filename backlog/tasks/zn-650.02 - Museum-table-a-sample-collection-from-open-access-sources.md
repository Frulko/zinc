## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
A small real collection that makes the demo convincing: about a dozen photographs of works and one or two 3D models from open-access, CC0 or public-domain sources (museum open-access programmes), resized and decimated for the repository (the whole example under 8 MB), each with its source, author, date and licence recorded in the project file and in a LICENSES file; texts in French and English. (Owner request, 2026-10-10: a demo app useful for mediation in museums: an interactive presentation on a touch table, photo gestures, 3D models, descriptions, presentations, in the manner of Klynt.)

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done (parallel worktree, 2026-10-10). Test: `tests/run --tier t1 --only museum_assets` -> PASS museum_assets (and museum_format still PASS).

The collection: "The Floating World, from Edo to Paris" (ukiyo-e prints, their echo in European painting, a Kangxi vase from Whistler's Peacock Room)
- 12 photographs, 1280 px on the long side, JPEG quality 70 (sips), 3.86 MB: nine from The Metropolitan Museum of Art (Hokusai: Great Wave JP1847, Red Fuji JP9, Ejiri JP12; Hiroshige: Sudden Shower JP643, Evening Snow at Kanbara JP2492; Utamaro: Three Beauties JP1528; Van Gogh: Irises 58.187; Toulouse-Lautrec: Divan Japonais 58.621.17; Whistler: Nocturne 17.3.159) and three from the Art Institute of Chicago (Hiroshige: Plum Garden at Kameido 1925.3752; Monet: Water Lily Pond 1933.441; Cassatt: The Letter 1932.1282).
- 1 model: baluster vase F1980.191a-c, National Museum of Asian Art (Smithsonian Open Access, CC0): the 149 999-triangle OBJ decimated to 12 000 triangles / 7 058 vertices (1.03 MB) with tools/decimate.cpp on the vendored meshoptimizer 1.3 (relative error 0.00055), texture 4096 -> 1536 px (0.58 MB, sips).
- 1 panorama: Ninomaru Teien (Nagoya Castle garden), Greg Zaal, Poly Haven, CC0, 2048x1024 (0.67 MB).
- Made for the example (CC0): video/great-wave.mp4 (10 s across the Met's Great Wave, ffmpeg, 854x480, 0.35 MB) with fr/en WebVTT; audio/welcome.{fr,en}.mp3, a placeholder note (7 KB each), with fr/en WebVTT written by tools/narration.sh --no-voice.
- Texts in French and English written from the museums' records: title, date, medium, description and audio description for each work; three text panels (Hokusai's postscript, Van Gogh's letter of 15 July 1888, the Peacock Room).
- examples/museum-table/LICENSES.md lists every file with its work, institution, accession number, credit line, source URL and licence; the same credits are in project.json. tools/fetch-collection.sh rebuilds every file from the sources; tools/narration.sh makes the narration with `say` from the transcripts.

Acceptance criteria
- #1 every asset has its source URL and licence in LICENSES and in the project file: museum_assets checks that each file of assets/ is named by the project and listed in LICENSES.md; the loader refuses a file-backed medium without credit.source and credit.licence (museum_format, no-licence.json).
- #2 under 8 MB: the example directory is 6756538 bytes at this commit (6.44 MB).
- #3 the model loads in zinc:3d and the photos in zinc:gfx: src/assets.ts prints `models/vase.obj: model 7058 vertices, 12000 triangles`, `photos/great-wave.jpg: image 1280x860` ... `22 files, all read` (tests/data/museum_assets/expected.txt); 0.9 s for the run, OBJ parsing included.

How each licence was checked (2026-10-10)
- The Met: `isPublicDomain: true` in the object record of the collection API for each of the nine. Objects where the API says false were left out even though they are old: Monet's Bridge over a Pond of Water Lilies (437127), Garden at Sainte-Adresse, and all Cassatt colour prints.
- Art Institute of Chicago: `is_public_domain: true` per artwork; the API states CC0 for its data except the `description` field (CC BY), which was not copied.
- Smithsonian: the Open Access record of F1980.191a-c gives "Restrictions & Rights: CC0" and CC0 on the 3d_voyager media.
- Poly Haven: the asset page states CC0 1.0.

Decisions
- Narration: nothing generated by `say` is committed (as we read Apple's licence, system voices are for personal, non-commercial use; to verify by the owner). The sample holds a neutral placeholder sound; tools/narration.sh generates voice and subtitles locally. Tried: 17.6 s per language, 88 KB each at 40 kbit/s MP3.
- Audio as MP3 (miniaudio decodes it; small). If zinc:audio (ZN-390) reads WAV/OGG only, change one ffmpeg line of narration.sh and the file names in the project.
- The Smithsonian model is the vase body (40.5 cm in the file; the museum gives 46.3 cm assembled with its cover).
- The Met search API v1 was retired on 2026-10-01; v1.1 is used. The AIC image server needs the `AIC-User-Agent` header and serves 1686 px wide images.

- The points of interest of the vase have provisional positions until ZN-650.04 shows the model.
