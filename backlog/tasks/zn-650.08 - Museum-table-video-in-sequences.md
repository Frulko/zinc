What was built (examples/museum-table/src):
- `film.ts` (new): a `Film` is a clip played through zinc:video, with its subtitles (the cues of its WebVTT file in the visitor's language, else its transcript at a reading pace: `cuesOf` in narration.ts, shared with the narration). The decoder is made once per file, at the size of the poster (zinc:video fits the clip in it). The clip is given to zinc:video as a file: `files.onDisk` copies the asset to the temporary directory once per run (an app's assets have no path; the editor's preview gives the file of the project folder).
- `table.ts`: a video medium is a card whose picture is the film. It plays by itself while its card is there, from its beginning each time the card comes (the items of a sequence have times); a touch pauses it and starts it again (a film has no full view); the film that has ended and does not loop shows its poster and the sign again. Over the picture: a timeline at the foot, what the film says above it (at the visitor's text size), and while it does not play a disc with a triangle. A film without a poster has a dark 16:9 screen.
- `player.ts`: the sequences on the table change through one door (`show`): the ones that leave, or wait under another one or under the attract loop, rest, which stops their films. Story lines: `film <id> play|pause|stop|end` and `subtitle <text>`.
- The sample: the poster of the film is now its first picture (`video/great-wave.jpg`, made by tools/fetch-collection.sh; 73 KB), so the card has the shape of the film (16:9) instead of the print's.

The criteria:
- #1: tests/data/museum_video/film.input, on the sequence of the film: `film.story` (play at frame 0, pause at 243, play at 333, the last sentence at 522 instead of 432: the film waited 90 frames, stop at 563 with the link back) and golden/museum-video/frames.txt (150: playing, 300: paused); compiled, the paused frame has the interpreter's hash.
- #2: `103 language en` is followed by `104 subtitle The foam ends in claws.`, the sentence being said, in English; museum_mode's idle.story has the same the other way at the reset.
- #3: the project names the source and the licence of the clip (The Met, CC0 1.0), LICENSES.md lists the clip, its poster and its subtitles (checked by museum_video and by museum_assets: 91 files).

A deterministic run decodes nothing: the engine gives it the stand-in of zinc:video (the plugin is `live`), so the goldens show the poster under the timeline and the subtitle. The real decoder is proven by tests/data/museum_video/seen.ts (ZINC_NATIVE=real, FFmpeg present on this Mac): "wave-film: pictures, its time runs, 10.0 s"; a real run of the table (not deterministic, headless, 200 frames) was looked at once. Outside a deterministic run `zinc run examples/museum-table` takes the real decoder by itself when FFmpeg's libraries are there.

Not proven: the film's picture inside the card in a window over several seconds (seen.ts proves the Film class, a screenshot of a fast headless run its first picture); a clip that does not loop, to its end, with the real decoder; a machine without FFmpeg (the code path is the stand-in's, which the tests run).

Decisions (for the coordinator to record):
- A film plays by itself when its card comes, as a Klynt sequence plays its video, and a touch pauses it: no play button to find, and a presentation or the attract loop shows it moving.
- Its subtitles are on its picture, not at the visitor's edge with the narration's: a sequence can have a narration and a film, and the film's words belong to the film.
- A film has no sound: zinc:video decodes pictures only. A sound track would be an audio medium of the sequence (its narration).
