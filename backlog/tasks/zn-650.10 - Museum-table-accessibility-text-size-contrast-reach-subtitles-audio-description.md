## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
(Owner's answers, 2026-10-10, for the showcase demos of M27.) A text-size setting and a high-contrast theme one touch away; every control reachable from the edge of the table (a visitor in a wheelchair, a child); subtitles for every narration and video; an audio description of each image, object and panorama, read by the synthesised voice on request.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done 2026-10-11 by two parallel agents (the second one finished from the first one's handover notes). Tests, all against main's binary (51d4809e): `PASS museum_access` (31 to 40 s beside two other building trees), `PASS museum_table`, `PASS museum_viewer`, `PASS museum_assets`, `PASS museum_mode`, `PASS museum_visit`, `PASS museum_editor`, `PASS museum_panorama`, `PASS museum_format`, `PASS contrast`.

What was built (examples/museum-table/src):
- Text size and high contrast: two buttons in the dock, left of the languages (`A A A`, one letter per size of `accessibility.textSizes`, the current one bright; a half disc). `type.ts` holds the reading state (`setReading(factor, highContrast)`, `reading()`, `faint(alpha)`, `clipped()`); the player sets it at the start of `frame`/`update`/`draw`/`setLanguage` and puts 1 back at the end, so the editor and other players are not affected. The colours are one `Colours` object the player owns and fills from the theme or from `highContrast`; in high contrast `faint()` makes translucent texts and label backgrounds opaque. Both settings go back to rest with the visit (`reset()`).
- Layout at the visitor's edge (`button.ts`, new): the dock is a left group and a right group, in one row when they fit, else the settings at the edge and the rest above. A sequence's links stay where designed while they fit; when they would leave the frame, touch each other or touch a dock button, every button of the sequence comes to the edge in centred rows. Under the storyboard the sequence keeps its layout.
- The buttons of the object and the panorama are stage buttons, with `describe <id>`, `next <id>` ("Next point": opens the points in turn) and `follow <id>` (the twin of an open point's panel button).
- Cards keep their footprint: a caption longer than designed (two title lines and one line under them, at the text size) takes its room from the picture, down to half (`Card.shrink`); at the designed size with the designed lines the card is exactly as before (the rule of the first handover shifted every picture by a few units: fixed, and frame 40 of museum_table then differs from main only in the box of the two new dock buttons, 1440..1720 x 1002..1058).
- Subtitles: above whatever is at the edge and above any button of the sequence they would cover; over the foot of the picture when a print is seen in full; as many lines as needed.
- Full view: the dock becomes `Refermer` + `Audiodescription` + the settings; the notice column widens with the text size.
- Audio descriptions: `accessibility.descriptions` (new optional format field: a path pattern with `{id}` and `{lang}`, default none; subtitles beside the clip as `.vtt`); `spokenDescription(project, medium)` in project.ts; `tools/narration.sh --only narration|descriptions` made the 28 clips and 28 subtitle files of `assets/audio/descriptions/` with Piper (1.6 MB; the example is 9.5 MB). Story lines: `text size N`, `contrast on|off`, `description <id> <clip>`, `description stop|end`, `point <id> of <medium>`.
- `--controls` (main.ts): one line whenever it changes, `controls <side> size <n> cut <texts cut short> name@x,y ...` in table units.
- `tools/contrast-check --museum project.json` (shared tool, default mode unchanged, `PASS contrast`): six pairs of the theme at 4.5:1 and of the high contrast at 7:1.

The criteria, on the output of `tests/t1/museum_access.sh`:
- #1: `golden/museum-access/sizes.txt` (the prints at the largest text, the largest text in high contrast, the designed and the middle size in high contrast), `object.txt`, `reach.txt`, `voice.txt`; every `controls` line of the four sessions says `cut 0` (the awk check fails on any other value). The frames were looked at.
- #2: the same check: no control farther than `table.reach` (520) from the nearest edge, and no button farther than it from the visitor's own edge, at four sides and three sizes; every control the sessions should lay out was seen.
- #3: `heard.ts` prints "28 audio descriptions, all heard" (14 media, two languages: the clip decodes, lasts what its subtitles say within 0.3 s, the mixer gives sound; run without ZINC_DETERMINISTIC, which decodes no sound); `voice.story` has the clips asked by the scripted touches (`description kanbara-snow audio/descriptions/kanbara-snow.fr.mp3`, the same in English after the language button, `description stop`, `description vase ...en.mp3` to `description end`). museum_assets lists the 56 files and "14 pictures, objects and panoramas, each with an audio description in fr and en".
- #4: `contrast-check --museum` passes the six pairs of the high contrast (lowest 14.67) and of the theme (lowest 6.25), and refuses grey on black.

The test: it took 16 minutes as first written because it asked for the hash of every frame (`ZINC_FRAMEHASH=all` costs a tenth of a second per 1920x1080 frame). It now plays each session to the frames it needs with `ZINC_FRAMEHASH=last` (the 9040 frames of the reset check take 3 s), on the interpreter; compiled, one frame (the largest text in high contrast) must have the interpreter's hash.

Goldens moved by the two new dock buttons, each compared old against new with tools/pngdiff before it was rewritten: museum_table (4 frames; poses unchanged), museum_viewer (3 frames, the vase's row has two more buttons; view log unchanged), museum_visit (11 frames; stories unchanged; besides the dock, a title of three lines now takes its third line from the picture instead of being cut), museum_mode (sides.txt, two.txt; stories unchanged; a subtitle no longer lies on the object's buttons), museum_editor (preview-320, window-90-w1, settings-48: the frames that show the player), museum_assets (56 files more). `docs/img/museum-table-access.jpg` is frame 170 of object.input.

Decisions (for the coordinator to record):
- The clips of the audio descriptions are named by one pattern in `accessibility` rather than by a field on every medium: one line in the file, nothing for the editor to add, and a museum replaces the clips under the same names; the id goes through the path check.
- The text size is a state of type.ts set around each frame of a player, not a parameter of some eighty `sans()` calls.
- At a larger text size a card keeps its place and size and gives picture room to its caption; growing cards covered their neighbours and the buttons.
- The audio description of a print is in its full view (the dock), that of an object or a panorama under it.
- "Next point" and the twin of a panel's button exist so that a visitor who cannot reach the middle of the table gets to every point of interest.
- The high contrast is judged at 7:1 (WCAG AAA), the theme at 4.5:1.

Known limits, written in the README: at the two larger sizes the buttons take two or three rows and lie over the foot of the lowest cards; the back of a card shows what fits. Estimated and not measured: secondary text on a card (ink at 63 % on paper) is about 4.3:1 in the default theme; it is opaque in high contrast.

Not proven: the GL renderer of a window at the larger sizes (the frames are the software raster's); a real table (touch, reach in centimetres).
