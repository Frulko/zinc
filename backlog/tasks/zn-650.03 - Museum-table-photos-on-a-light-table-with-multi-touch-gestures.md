## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Photos lie on the table as cards: one finger drags, two fingers pinch to zoom and rotate, with inertia and soft edges; several cards and several hands at the same time, each gesture owning its card; tap brings to front, double tap opens full view with the caption and the description, a flip shows the back with the text. On the multitouch of zinc:gfx (touchCount, touchId) and plugins/gestures; mouse and trackpad as a fallback. (Owner request, 2026-10-10: a demo app useful for mediation in museums: an interactive presentation on a touch table, photo gestures, 3D models, descriptions, presentations, in the manner of Klynt.)

What exists
- examples/museum-table/src/main.ts: the player. Loads assets/project.json, lays the current sequence on the table, routes every finger to what it landed on (the full view, a button, a card), draws the heading, the cards, the buttons to the other sequences and the language button. `-- --sequence <id> --lang <code> --log --stress <n>`.
- src/touch.ts: Fingers (touch points, or the mouse as one finger; landing, lifting, tap) and manipulate(pose, fingers): one finger drags, two turn and zoom about their midpoint, from the fingers' motion since the previous frame only.
- src/table.ts: LightTable and Card: ownership by landing, bring to front, glide after release (speed kept, decay 4 per second), soft edges (the centre eases back inside the table), the folded corner turns a photograph over to its notice (title, creator, date, medium, description, credit), a double tap opens the full view; text media are cards too; a film shows its poster and a panorama its flat image with a note until their tasks.
- src/type.ts (fonts from a ladder of sizes, word wrap), src/words.ts (the player's own few words, fr / en), zinc.json.
- next/tests/t1/museum_table.sh, tests/data/museum_table/ (hands.py writes hands.input; expected.log), tests/golden/museum-table/frames.txt.

Decisions (to record by the coordinator)
- Cards are drawn with zinc:gfx under setTransform (ZN-263.01, on main the same day), not as textured quads of a zinc:3d scene. The first version was the zinc:3d one (the choice announced when zinc:gfx had no rotation): it worked and was measured (30 cards: 21 to 25 ms on the software renderer, 3.3 ms on the GPU one, headless) but had nearest texels, no alpha (a baked rim of table around each card, no shadow over another card), an image per card repainted at each zoom, and the limit of 64 runtime images. Under the matrix: anti-aliased edges, real shadows, texts set in the font of their size on screen (sharp at every zoom and angle), no image per card, 150 lines less. The cost is on the CPU raster, below.
- plugins/gestures is not used: it reads the first two touch points of the whole screen for one region; a table needs each finger owned by what it landed on. Fingers and manipulate (120 lines, src/touch.ts) do that; they are the candidates to move into the plugin.
- Gesture constants (table units, src/table.ts and src/touch.ts): tap under 0.35 s and 14 units; double tap within 0.45 s; card width between 150 and 1500; folded corner 46; glide decay 4 per second, turn decay 6; edges: centre kept 70 inside, eased at 9 per second. From the research: one finger never turns a card (visitors chain gestures and seldom rotate; RNT left out), no long press, buttons take touches in at least 150 by 76 units (75 mm on a 43-inch table, Smithsonian guideline).
- Without a touch screen: the mouse is one finger; the wheel and the trackpad pinch zoom the card under the pointer, Shift + wheel turns it.
- The heading (title and introduction of the sequence) is written on the table under the cards; buttons are over everything.
- Pixel goldens are frame hashes (exact, a few bytes) rather than PNG files: four 1920x1080 photographs would weigh about 10 MB. Each hash is the last frame of a run cut at that frame, because hashing every frame rasterizes all of them (below).

Measured, for the engine (machine loaded)
- Software raster of transformed commands: 115 ms per frame for the 8 cards of the sample at 1920x1080, 330 ms for 30 cards (`ZINC_FRAMEHASH=all`, 160 frames in 19 s). Fine for captures and goldens, not for a table without a GPU.
- Start of the player: 0.9 to 1.3 s, of which the decoding of the 15 images of the assets at every start.
