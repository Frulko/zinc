## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
What a table needs and a screen does not: panels and texts turn toward the side of the table the touch came from; one or two visitors share the table and one story; large targets; an attract loop when nobody touches, and a reset to the start after a set idle time; kiosk behaviour (fullscreen, no way out by gesture, errors never shown to visitors). (Owner request, 2026-10-10: a demo app useful for mediation in museums: an interactive presentation on a touch table, photo gestures, 3D models, descriptions, presentations, in the manner of Klynt.)

Owner's answers, 2026-10-10: ONE OR TWO visitors at a time sharing one story (not independent zones); texts and panels turn toward the side of the last touch, photo cards stay free; fullscreen without a way out and errors never shown; attract loop after 60 s and reset after 120 s by default, both in the project file.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
2026-10-10, done in one session by a parallel agent (worktree-agent-ae16543f7ac5df688), after ZN-650.05.
What was built. (1) The visitor's frame (src/frame.ts): texts, link buttons, the player's controls, subtitles, the presentation's timeline, the storyboard, the full view, the panels and labels of the object viewer and of the panorama are laid out in frame units and drawn under one matrix (zinc:gfx setTransform, ZN-263.01) for the right, far and left sides; at the main side nothing is transformed (the frames of museum_viewer kept their hashes when the viewer moved into the frame, before the marks at the edges were added). At a short side the frame is 1080 by 1920: the link buttons are set in centred rows above the player's own, the full view puts the notice under the picture, the storyboard fits itself. The 3D object stands upright for the visitor (the camera's up vector follows the side; drags, the pan beside an open panel and the projection of the points follow), the cards stay free. (2) Turning: a tap, or a press on a button, within min(table.reach, 15 % of the table's depth) = 162 units of an edge turns everything to that edge, at the end of the frame so that the touch itself is read in the frame it was made in; a mark in the middle of each other edge says 'touch here to turn the table to you'. (3) Idle: after kiosk.attractAfter the presentation kiosk.attract plays again and again without sound (subtitles only) over the visit, with 'Touch the table to begin' at the four edges; a touch gives the visit back as it was; after kiosk.resetAfter the visit is forgotten (start sequence, default language, main side, nothing seen, narrations to be said again) and a touch starts a new one. A presentation a visitor watches does not count as idleness. Story log lines for ZN-650.12: 'visit start', 'visit end', 'attract start', 'attract stop', 'reset: ...', 'side ...'. (4) Kiosk: Escape is taken by the app when kiosk.fullscreen (escapeByApp); an overlay without a link back gets a 'Close' button; a project that cannot be read gives an empty table and one line on the error output; a fault in a frame is logged, the table goes back to its first sequence, and to an empty table after three faults in a row.
Decisions. (a) The side is not guessed from every touch: a first version turned on any tap within 30 % of the depth of an edge, and the scripted visit of ZN-650.05 showed the fault at once (a tap on the hotspot of a card lying 312 units from the far edge turned the table away from the visitor who made it). The strip is now the edge itself (15 %, 9 cm on a 43-inch table) and a drag never turns: nobody loses the texts by moving a card or reaching across; the cost is that the second visitor touches their edge once (the mark is there for that) before a panel opens their way. (b) One frame for everything, not one orientation per panel: two visitors share one story (owner), and a panel that stayed turned to the other side while the buttons moved would split it. (c) The attract loop is silent: an empty room is left in peace. (d) kiosk.fullscreen does not make the window fullscreen by itself: the engine does that from ZINC_KIOSK=1 or zinc.json ("kiosk": true), which would trap a developer who runs the demo; README says how to start it as a kiosk. (e) ZN-263 (UI style transforms), a dependency of this task, was not needed: the table draws with zinc:gfx, whose matrix is ZN-263.01.
