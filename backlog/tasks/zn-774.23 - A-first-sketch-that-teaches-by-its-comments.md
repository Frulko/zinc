## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Research (links): Arduino Blink (https://github.com/arduino/arduino-examples/blob/main/examples/01.Basics/Blink/Blink.ino): a header that says the outcome, one plain comment per block saying when it runs, a comment per action so the number to change is obvious. NN/g empty states and learning cues (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/). Takeaway kept: under 15 lines of code, every knob named with its effect and a value to try.
Done (AC1, AC3): starters/drawing.ts is the Drawing style's first result, a circle that follows the pointer and grows while pressed; its comments are '// @t <key>' markers that newSketch() expands into the interface language (i18n/<lang>/atelier.json atelier.first.*), four marked ✎ places (background colour, the clear line for a trail, radius, colour and opacity). The start view opens with the card 'Your first sketch' (Start.tsx FirstCard, openFirst -> newSketch('drawing', 'first-sketch'), session command 'first'); the Atelier tour's example step points at it. docs/start/styles.md (and its four translations) gains the row 'First result' per style: Drawing a circle that follows the pointer, Scene a ball that changes colour when tapped, Canvas a breathing circle in the middle, Interface a number and a +1 button (the four starters). tests/t0/atelier_first.sh (6 s) runs the four starters headless with a scripted pointer and checks pixels and the counter's text, and that every @t key has its five texts. Checked by hand: an Atelier session (first, lang:fr, first) wrote first-sketch/main.ts in English then French; zinc check app/atelier ok.
Handover (AC2 open): the agreed results are in the start view, the Atelier tour and styles.md, not yet in zinc new's style templates (game, stage, canvas, desktop-app are full projects: a game, a sky with a star) nor in the first lesson of each style (01, 07, 13, 17 and their samples, goldens, site play pages, translations). Decision to take next: either the templates and lessons open on the agreed result (rewrite four lessons in five languages), or the agreed results are changed to what lessons 01/07/13/17 already draw.
