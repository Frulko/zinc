## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Done 2026-10-10. bench/gl_calls.ts (zinc:gl, interpreted or built) and bench/gl_calls.js (QuickJS): 200k calls each of getError (the binding alone), uniform4f, uniformMatrix4fv (16 floats) and a 3-index drawElements; numbers in docs/reports/zinc-next-perf.md. The HostCall decoding was about 40% of a uniform call, so the scalar rows (getError, uniform1i/1f/3f/4f, drawArrays, drawElements) got direct entries: zn_webgl_fast in src/gl/webgl_host.cpp fills zn::host::hostFast when zinc loads libzn_webgl or when an AOT program's installGl runs; the AOT emitter takes them for gl rows as for gfx rows. AOT: getError 40 -> 17 ns, uniform4f 93 -> 65, drawElements 360 -> 330; interpreted 50 -> 28, 110 -> 80. zinc:gl beats QuickJS on every call; QuickJS's uniformMatrix4fv costs 1.8 us (ZN-567). Tests: gl_cube (interpreter = AOT), host_direct, aot. usage: n/a
