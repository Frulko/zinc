## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Second part of ZN-590. The GL replay rasterizes every LINE/POLY on the CPU into a coverage tile (draw_poly): navigation 610 tiles per frame, 111 ms of CPU per frame on the Intel Mac (ZN-590 notes). Draw them on the GPU: the contours as triangle fans into the stencil (nonzero: incr/decr wrap, even-odd: invert), a 1 px anti-aliased fringe outside the fill, then a cover quad (NanoVG's technique, docs/reports/gpu-renderer-design.md); the canvas gradient paint (grad 4) in the shader. Desktop GL and the GLES2 prelude (D29).
