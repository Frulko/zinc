## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Criterion 1 (9cf7643b): zinc:stage's Stage gains `update(dt)`, the first half of `frame` (pointer and wheel routed to the zones, onUpdate, flings; `frame` now calls it), and `drawIn(x, y, w, h)`, which lays out and draws the scene in a window box: the stage's corner is the box's (stage.x/y, so mouseX and the zones count from it; stage.width/height its size), the drawing is clipped to it and the zones are cut to it (the scroll box's cut, now `cutZonesTo`; `endScrollBox` restores the box's cut), `stage.color` is not painted (the Canvas's own background). A scene inside an interface: `<Canvas onDraw={(x, y, w, h) => stage.drawIn(x, y, w, h)} />` and `render(App, bg, (dt) => stage.update(dt))`. An interface inside a scene needs no new API: a full-window Canvas draws the scene, zinc:ui elements are placed `absolute` over a sprite (two signals copied from the sprite's x/y after `stage.update` in render's frame function), and the sprite registers a no-op tap zone under them so the scene behind takes no click. lib/std/ui.ts untouched.
Proof: tests/golden/stage (T0 `stage`, interpreter and QuickJS): `drawIn(100, 50, 60, 40)` puts a child at 110,98; a hit inside the box finds `body`, the same node beyond the box and a child below it find none (both are hit without the cut); mouseX/Y 10 2 count from the box; a zone registered after drawIn is not cut. Samples run by learn_samples with scripted clicks: docs/start/samples/scene-in-interface (Add a fish is a zinc:ui button, a click on a fish feeds it through its tap zone) and interface-in-scene (a tap pops a balloon, the sign is dragged by its bar and the interface follows it, its button over a balloon adds one and pops none); the same lines on `--engine quickjs` by hand.
Criterion 2: docs/start/beyond.md, seven sections, each a sample in docs/start/samples/ with its picture in docs/start/img/ (captured headless with its scripted clicks, each looked at): chart-gfx (Canvas onDraw with zinc:gfx), chart-canvas (the same chart with zinc:canvas: bezier curve, gradient fill), style-objects (StyleSheet.create, style arrays, a dynamic width and colour; no className, so its ZBC has no `func @applyToken`), own-components (props, children, a component's own signal, an onChange callback), animation (transition classes on colours and a translateX, Animated.sequence of timing and spring with a done callback), scene-in-interface, interface-in-scene. Linked from docs/start/styles.md ("How they mix": the two new mixes replace "a stage is the whole window"), docs/learn/README.md, lessons 10, 11, 12, 16 and 20, and docs/reference/stage.md. tests/t1/learn_samples.sh runs the seven with their inputs (tests/data/learn_samples/<sample>.input), checks that the page holds each sample word for word and that docs/start/samples holds no unchecked sample; tests/affected.json already maps docs/start/ and lib/std/stage.ts to it.
