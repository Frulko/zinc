## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Clicking a building opens a parchment window with its model, state in plain words (working, no road, no workers, missing input), workers, stock and what it needs; clicking a person shows role, job and carried load. Right click or Escape closes; windows can be dragged.
