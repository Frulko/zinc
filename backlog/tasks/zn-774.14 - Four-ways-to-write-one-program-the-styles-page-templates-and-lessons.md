- Canvas full window with onFrame and no JSX already worked (docs/plugins/canvas2d.md now shows it). Gap found and fixed: under zinc:gfx setTransform (inside a zinc:stage sprite, or a turned zinc:ui parent) the native fill() and image() of plugins/canvas2d ignored gfx::matrix while the clip and gfx's own primitives followed it, so shapes, gradients and images landed at the wrong place and were cut away. The fix applies the matrix to the points, the gradient geometry and the image corners (images are placed, not turned). Regression: tests/t1/canvas.sh draws one scene moved by setTransform and one placed by hand, with ZINC_NATIVE=real, and tools/pngdiff must find them equal (3588 then 13672 differing pixels without the fix).
- Tests (worktree build/zinc): learn_samples, canvas, templates, templates_kickstart, first_run, i18n, cli_core, templates_index, image_rect, gfx_transform, stage: all PASS. `tests/run --changed` maps to the whole tier (src/cli_core.cpp), so the tests above were picked by hand. The styles samples, the eight lesson samples and both templates also run on --engine quickjs with the same output; AOT not run.
